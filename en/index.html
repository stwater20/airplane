Fly from Taipei to Tokyo in five minutes

“Fly to Tokyo” is a free 3D flight game that runs in your browser. Board your passengers at Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport, take off through a fire-truck water salute, steer through golden rings along the Ryukyu Islands and the southern coast of Japan, then follow the ILS down to runway 34L at Tokyo Narita.

📱 One-thumb controlsDrag anywhere, use the on-screen D-pad, or tilt your phone. Takeoff and landing are automatic.
🔥 Combos & PERFECTsChain rings for up to ×5, hit the center for PERFECT, grab blue boost rings.
😊 Passenger comfortSmooth flying keeps passengers happy. Ride out turbulence without overcorrecting.
🗺️ Real terrainElevation from USGS SRTM/GMTED2010 and NOAA ETOPO1; ground colour from NASA Blue Marble.
🎙️ Radio & cabinTower clearances with pilot readbacks, a flight-attendant safety briefing and captain announcements.
🎰 Liveries & daily challengeCollect liveries from the SecTools Airlines gacha and compare scores on the daily route.

About the route

Taipei Taoyuan (TPE) to Tokyo Narita (NRT) is roughly 2,180 km (about 1,180 nautical miles) and usually takes around three hours. In the game, cruise is fast-forwarded so a full flight takes about four to five minutes.
